Research overview

Learning from recordings and images of the brain.

01

Learning across subjects and tasks

My ongoing work focuses on foundation models and data scaling for human intracranial recordings, with the goal of learning representations that transfer across people and tasks. I’m building models for neural time series and video data for brain-machine interfaces, building on my work on WiCAT for widefield calcium imaging.

02

Decoding movement and cognitive states

I’m developing preferential models to learn brain dynamics relevant to behavior, such as movement and cognitive states. My work spans SBIND for neural images and forward-backward filtering and smoothing for neural time series.
